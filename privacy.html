Privacy Policy

How Agentic collects, uses, stores and deletes the information you share with our AI agents.

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. What data we collect

We keep collection limited to what is needed to operate your account and run the agents you request.

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Google account identifier: when you choose Sign in with Google, we receive the basic identifier needed to connect your Google sign-in to your Agentic account.
  • Profile avatar: the animated avatar you select in Account Settings.
  • Credit balance and transaction history: free signup credits, purchased credits, and credits spent on agent runs.
  • User-submitted agent parameters: the stock brief preferences you choose (such as tickers and watchlist settings) or the B2B Lead Scraper search inputs you submit (such as target country, search terms and selected Excel columns).

2. How we use your data

  • Authentication: to create and secure your account, sign you in, and manage your session.
  • Credits: to manage free and purchased credits, keep your balance accurate, and record agent-related transactions.
  • Agent delivery: to deliver the outputs you request — SaaS Daily Stock Brief emails and B2B Lead Scraper Excel results — to your delivery email address.
  • Password resets: to send 6-digit email verification codes when you request a password reset.

3. Data storage and third parties

Account data and credit records are stored securely in Neon Serverless Postgres. We use hosting and email infrastructure only to operate the service and deliver the outputs you request.

Google Sign-In is used only for basic identity verification — specifically your email address and profile name. We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or any other Google data.

4. Data deletion and contact

You can request deletion of your account or associated data at any time by emailing ngyukseng2004@gmail.com. Please send the request from the email address associated with your Agentic account so we can verify ownership.

We will action verified deletion requests within a reasonable period, except where limited data must be retained to meet legal, security or fraud-prevention obligations.
