About Agentic

We built the analyst you can't afford to hire.

Agentic started as a spreadsheet, a cron job and two people who were tired of scrolling charts at 6am.

Our story

In 2024 we were running a small trading desk with a big problem: the information that actually mattered — verified price action, momentum, and the two or three headlines that explain a gap — arrived in five different places at five different times. By the time it was assembled, the pre-market window was gone.

So we automated it. What began as a nightly script that emailed us a single NVDA brief turned into a network of autonomous agents that every morning at 07:00 MYT do the boring, disciplined work of reading the tape properly.

Today Agentic is a pay-as-you-go marketplace. You buy credits, you deploy an agent, and you get seven days of pre-market intelligence at a price that scales with your watchlist — not your seat count.

Why pre-market intelligence?

The first 30 minutes of a US session are where retail traders lose the most edge. Briefs that arrive after the open are entertainment. We wanted a brief you could read with your first coffee — before the market gives you the answer for free.

What we will never do

  • Tell you to buy or sell a specific position size
  • Execute a trade or touch custody of your money
  • Publish a headline we haven't matched to a source
  • Hide a recurring charge behind a free trial

Delivery promise

07:00 MYT, every day of your 7-day window. If a data feed is late, the agent waits for verified candles rather than shipping a guess.

Data accuracy standards

Boring inputs. Trustworthy outputs.

Every number in a brief can be traced back to a rule below.

01

Verified OHLC candles

Daily open/high/low/close series are validated for missing sessions, split adjustments, duplicate prints and stale ticks before use.

02

12/26/9 MACD momentum engine

The standard 12-period EMA vs 26-period EMA spread, smoothed by a 9-period signal line. Bias is expressed as Buy, Hold or Sell — never as a price target.

03

Curated news synthesis

Three headlines per ticker, each matched to a named source and timestamp, then condensed into one plain-English paragraph without editorial spin.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do credits work?

Credits are prepaid units of monitoring. One stock for seven days costs 5 credits. A 2-stock watchlist costs 10 credits per activation, a 3-stock watchlist costs 15, and so on. Credits never expire while your account is active and there is no auto-renewal.

What time does the brief arrive?

Every day at 07:00 MYT (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur, UTC+8) for the full length of your 7-day window. The email is built mobile-first, so it reads well on a phone without zooming.

Which stocks can I monitor?

A curated set of liquid US equities and ETFs: NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, PLTR, SPY, MSFT, AMZN, META, GOOGL, AMD, QQQ and NFLX. The configuration grid only lights up tickers your credit balance can afford.

Can I change my watchlist mid-week?

Each activation holds its own fixed 7-day watchlist so the brief stays consistent. To change tickers, let the current window run out — or activate a second window with the new selection once you have enough credits.

What happens if I have fewer than 5 credits?

The Subscribe button on the Stock Market News Agent stays locked until you hold at least 5 credits. Top up in Manage My Plan — a $5 Starter Top-Up is exactly enough for one stock for one week.

Is this financial advice?

No. Agentic produces informational and educational summaries for tracking purposes only. Nothing produced by an agent is a recommendation, solicitation or personalised advice. Always do your own research.
