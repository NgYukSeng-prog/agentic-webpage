About Agentic

The analyst you can't afford to hire.

Built by two traders who were tired of 6am chart scrolling.

Our story

The data that mattered lived in five places at five different times. By the time we assembled it, the pre-market window was gone.

So we automated it: nightly scripts became autonomous agents that do the boring, disciplined reading for us — every morning at 07:00 MYT.

Agentic is pay-as-you-go on purpose. Buy credits, deploy an agent, get results.

Why pre-market?

Briefs that arrive after the open are entertainment. Ours arrive before the answer is free.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do credits work?

One stock for seven days costs 5 credits. The Lead Scraper costs 3 credits per 50-lead term (max 10 terms). Credits never expire and never auto-renew.

When does the brief arrive?

Every day at 07:00 MYT for the length of your 7-day window. Mobile-first, no zooming.

Which stocks can I monitor?

NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, PLTR, SPY, MSFT, AMZN, META, GOOGL, AMD, QQQ and NFLX. The grid only unlocks what you can afford.

How does the Lead Scraper deliver?

It searches your 10 terms, stops at 1,000 unique leads, and builds an .xlsx with only the columns you picked.

What if I have fewer than 5 credits?

The Subscribe buttons stay locked. A $5 Starter Top-Up is exactly one stock for one week.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything is informational and educational tracking only. Always do your own research.
