About LiteWork

Digital workers, priced like a coffee run.

Low-cost AI help for everyday businesses — without the salaries or the subscriptions.

Our purpose

LiteWork exists for the people who run lean: solo founders, small teams and busy individuals who need real analytical and research work done, but cannot justify a full-time hire — and are tired of stacking expensive monthly SaaS subscriptions for tools they only touch once or twice a week.

So we built something simpler: a small roster of specialized digital workers you hire by the task. A morning market brief. A clean list of B2B leads. Real output, delivered to your inbox, for a few dollars.

How LiteWork helps

Everything runs on simple credits that never expire. You only pay for the exact work you need — whether that is a five-trading-day morning stock briefing for one ticker, or a 50–500 lead B2B Excel list pulled from your own search terms. No minimum term, no auto-renewal, and no credits burned on months you don't use us.

Top up once, then deploy a worker whenever a task lands on your desk. Unused credits sit quietly in your wallet until you need them.

Built for real output

You should not need prompt engineering to get value from AI. Pick your tickers or paste your search terms, choose the columns you want, and press go — configuration takes about 30 seconds. The worker does the reading, the scraping and the formatting, then delivers clean, ready-to-use results straight to your inbox.

No dashboards to babysit. No exports to clean up. Just the finished work.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do credits work?

One stock for five trading days (Mon–Fri) costs 5 credits. The Lead Scraper costs 3 credits per 50-lead term (max 10 terms). Credits never expire and never auto-renew.

When does the brief arrive?

Every market morning at 07:00 MYT for 5 trading days (Mon–Fri). Weekends are skipped while US markets are closed, so you always receive 5 full market-day briefs. Mobile-first, no zooming.

Which stocks can I monitor?

NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, PLTR, SPY, MSFT, AMZN, META, GOOGL, AMD, QQQ and NFLX. The grid only unlocks what you can afford.

How does the Lead Scraper deliver?

It searches your terms, stops at your chosen lead target (50–500), and builds an .xlsx with only the columns you picked.

What if I have fewer than 5 credits?

The Subscribe buttons stay locked. The $5 Starter pack (25 credits) covers five stocks for a full market week.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything is informational and educational tracking only. Always do your own research.
