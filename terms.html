The simple rules behind LiteWork credits, AI workers and the results we deliver.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Using LiteWork

LiteWork provides pay-as-you-go AI workers that complete defined back-office tasks — lead research, email enrichment, invoice-to-Excel OCR, competitor review analysis and daily stock briefs. You need a LiteWork account to launch any worker.

  • One credit wallet: every worker draws from the same credit balance.
  • Per-run pricing: the credit cost of each run is shown before you confirm, and credits are deducted when the run starts.
  • Accuracy: you are responsible for checking outputs before using them in your business.

2. Payments and credits

  • Pay once: credit packs are one-off USD purchases. There is no monthly subscription.
  • No auto-renewal: we never charge you again automatically or renew a plan without a new purchase.
  • Credits never expire: unused credits stay in your wallet until you spend them.
  • New accounts: every new account receives 5 free credits to test any worker.

3. What we never do

These commitments apply to every LiteWork worker and every plan:

  • No auto-renewal: no hidden recurring charges and no surprise renewals.
  • Credits never expire: we do not wipe or expire unused credits.
  • We never sell your files or data: uploaded documents, lead lists and results are used only to complete your requested run.
  • No financial advice: market briefs are informational and educational only — never position sizes or trade calls.

4. Market briefs are informational only

Stock Market News Agent output is provided for informational and educational tracking purposes only. It is not financial advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security. LiteWork Labs is not a broker, dealer, investment adviser or fiduciary.

You should always do your own research before making investment decisions.

5. Outputs and acceptable use

  • Delivery: completed runs produce an Excel file and/or email delivered to your account address.
  • No resale as a data broker: use extracted leads in line with applicable anti-spam and privacy laws.
  • Fair use: do not use LiteWork to scrape or process content you are not permitted to access.
